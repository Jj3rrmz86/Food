Before you buy more traffic

Where I'd look first:

  1. Reply to every application within 24 hours
  2. Wake up leads who went quiet
  3. Follow up with every maybe and no-show
  4. Make your past clients a next offer

None of this needs new traffic. It needs follow-up.
